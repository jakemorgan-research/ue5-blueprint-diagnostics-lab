# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, patient with beginners, and precise about evidence. Challenge a Blueprint explanation or engineering decision without attacking the person who proposed it.

Welcome contributions include reproducible cases, redacted Blueprint exports, clearer diagrams, version-specific package evidence, and focused fixes. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, spam, doxxing, credential disclosure, and redistribution of private or third-party assets are not accepted.

## Privacy and asset boundary

Do not post private project files, personal information, network addresses, credentials, signing material, Marketplace assets, device logs, executables, or APKs unless you own the material and have deliberately prepared it for public redistribution.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary. Use GitHub's built-in content-reporting controls for sensitive conduct concerns; do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where safe, and applied consistently.
